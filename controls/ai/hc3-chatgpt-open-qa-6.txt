In a theological sense, the term "rapture" refers to the belief that true believers in Jesus Christ will be bodily resurrected and taken up into heaven, or "raptured," at the end of the world or at the second coming of Christ. This belief is based on a interpretation of certain passages in the New Testament, particularly in the letters of Paul and in the book of Revelation.\n\nAccording to this belief, the rapture will happen suddenly and unexpectedly, and those who are "raptured" will be spared from experiencing the tribulations and suffering that will occur on earth during the end times. The exact timing and details of the rapture are a matter of debate among Christian theologians and scholars.\n\nIt should be noted that the concept of the rapture is not accepted by all Christian denominations and is not considered a core belief by all Christians. Some view the concept as being based on a misinterpretation of scripture, while others view it as being a modern, extra-biblical belief that has no basis in the teachings of Jesus or the early Christian church.
