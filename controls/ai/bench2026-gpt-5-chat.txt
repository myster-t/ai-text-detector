Good afternoon everyone,  

It’s a pleasure to be here today to talk about a topic that is shaping our world at an unprecedented pace — entrepreneurship.  

Entrepreneurship is more than starting a business; it’s about identifying opportunities, solving problems, and creating value. At its core, it’s about seeing gaps in the market and having the courage to act on them. This mindset is relevant to all of you, regardless of your academic discipline. Whether you study engineering, arts, science, or business, the ability to think like an entrepreneur can open doors to possibilities you may not yet imagine.  

In our rapidly changing global economy, the traditional career pathways are evolving. Technology, globalization, and shifting societal needs are constantly reshaping industries. This means that adaptability, creativity, and resilience are no longer optional — they’re essential. Entrepreneurs embrace change instead of fearing it, and they see challenges as opportunities to innovate.  

One of the most important aspects of entrepreneurship is problem-solving. The most successful ventures often start by addressing real-world problems — from improving healthcare delivery, to making education more accessible, to creating sustainable energy solutions. The path from idea to execution is rarely straightforward. It requires research, learning from failures, and iterating until you find what works.  

Equally important is networking and collaboration. Nobody succeeds entirely on their own. Building relationships with mentors, peers, and industry professionals can accelerate your growth. Entrepreneurship thrives in communities where ideas are exchanged freely, and where people support one another’s ambitions.  

While success stories can be inspiring, it’s important to remember that entrepreneurship is not about instant wins. It’s about persistence. Many entrepreneurs face setbacks, but they use those experiences as lessons to refine their approach. The difference between those who give up and those who succeed often lies in their willingness to keep going when things get tough.  

For university students, this is the perfect time to explore entrepreneurial thinking. You have access to knowledge, diverse perspectives, and resources that can help you test ideas in a safe environment. The sooner you start experimenting, the sooner you build the confidence and skills that will serve you for life.  

So, as you continue your academic journey, ask yourself: What problems in the world do I care about solving? What unique skills or insights do I bring to the table? And how can I take the first step toward making my ideas a reality?  

Entrepreneurship is not reserved for a select few — it’s a mindset that anyone can develop. And it’s a mindset that can empower you to shape not just your own future, but the future of the communities and industries you care about.  

Thank you.
