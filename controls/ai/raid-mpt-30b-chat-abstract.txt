This academic paper presents some results on the equivalence of multi-letter quantum finite automata, which are a generalization of classical finite automata that allow superposition states and entanglement. The authors introduce two notions of equivalence for these systems: structural invariance under unitary transformations and trace preservation while accepting specific input words. They show that both concepts coincide when restricted to deterministic machines with a single output symbol, but they may differ in general cases due to noncommutative effects originating from superpositions over different outputs or inputs simultaneously processed by nondeterministic configurations. Finally, as an application example, they discuss how this framework enables effective verification whether certain protocols based on continuous variable QKD (quantum key distribution) satisfy desired
