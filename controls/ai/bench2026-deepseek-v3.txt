Team,

If I could have your attention for a few minutes.

I want to talk to you directly about the changes we’re announcing today. I know the word “restructuring” can land heavily. It brings uncertainty, and with that, a natural feeling of anxiety. I want to acknowledge that feeling right now. It’s real, and it’s understandable.

But I also need you to hear this: this is not a step back. This is a necessary, strategic step forward to ensure our company not only survives but thrives in the future that’s coming.

The market has shifted. Our industry is evolving at a pace we’ve never seen. And to stand still, to do things the way we’ve always done them, is the greatest risk we could take. This restructuring is our choice to be bold. It’s our commitment to adapt, to become more agile, more focused, and ultimately, stronger.

Yes, this means some roles and teams will change. Some of our processes will be reinvented. This will be challenging. There will be days that feel difficult and unfamiliar. I am not going to sugarcoat that.

But here is what will not change: our core mission. The reason we all come to work. The problem we are here to solve for our customers. That remains. And more importantly, the most critical asset of this company remains: you. Your talent, your dedication, your resilience—that is the engine of this organization. This process is about clearing away the obstacles so that your talents can have an even greater impact.

We have a vision for where we are going. A future where we are more innovative, more efficient, and more connected to our customers' needs than ever before. Getting there will require every single one of us. It will require patience. It will require open minds. It will require us to support one another.

So, in the coming days and weeks, I ask for your courage. I ask for your trust. Lean on your teams. Communicate openly. Your managers and leaders are here to guide you, and my door is always open.

This is our moment to build the next chapter of this company, together. Let’s move forward with focus, with determination, and with the confidence that we have what it takes to succeed.

Thank you. Now, let’s get to work.
